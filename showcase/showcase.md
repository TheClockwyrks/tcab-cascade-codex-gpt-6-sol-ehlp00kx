Cascade is a quiet game of Klondike solitaire on a deep green felt table. Deal seven columns, uncover the hidden cards, and build four foundations from Ace to King.

Move cards by dragging them between columns. Colors alternate as the ranks descend, and an empty column welcomes a King. Double-click an exposed card to send it straight to its foundation when it fits.

The stock turns three cards at a time. Work from the front of the waste fan, and turn the empty stock to recycle the waste for another pass.

When the last card reaches home, the foundations erupt. Cards arc, bounce, and leave a growing collage across the felt before a new deal begins.
